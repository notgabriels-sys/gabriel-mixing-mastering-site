Fit check

This is for electronic tracks that need translation, low-end control, release handoff, or a clear second set of ears before distribution. It works best when the idea is already there and the job is to make the record hold together outside the room it was made in.

It is not a production-replacement service by default. Arrangement, sound design, vocal editing, restoration, rush delivery, artwork and distribution are separate conversations.

Send-ready checklist

For mastering

  • 24-bit WAV at the session sample rate.
  • No clipping, and no final limiter if possible.
  • The rough/loud version you have been checking against.
  • One or two references for tone, level and low-end behaviour.

For mixing

  • Consolidated stems or multitracks from the same start point.
  • Your rough mix, even if it is not perfect.
  • Notes for effects, edits or balances that must stay.
  • References and a clear release or feedback deadline.

Fastest way to get a useful answer

Send one private link, the service you are considering, your deadline, and what still feels unresolved in the track. If the wrong service is selected, it will be corrected before work starts.

How it works

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Send a rough mix or a private link, plus references and your deadline. You'll get a straight answer on whether it's a good fit and which service applies.

  2. 02

    Confirm scope and slot

    The files, rate, turnaround and delivery date are confirmed by email before the project starts. Some fixed services can be booked through hosted PayPal; the rest stay enquiry-first until the correct link and scope are confirmed.

  3. 03

    Send your files

    Reply to the project confirmation with a download link — WeTransfer, Dropbox, Drive, whatever you already use. Mixes as 24-bit WAV with headroom and no limiter on the master bus; stems all starting at the same point.

  4. 04

    First version back

    Within the turnaround stated on the service. You get the files plus a short note on what was done and why.

  5. 05

    Revisions, then delivery

    Send notes and get a revised version — the included passes are part of the price, not an upsell. Final files are delivered in every format the service lists.

Working to a fixed release date? Say so before booking — turnarounds are typical, not guaranteed, and a slot can usually be arranged around a deadline if there's notice.

Before you send

What should I send for mastering?

Send a 24-bit WAV at the session sample rate, with enough headroom to avoid clipping. If possible, remove the final limiter. Include the version you have been listening to, plus one or two references.

What should I send for mixing?

Send consolidated multitracks or stems that all begin at the same point, together with your rough mix. Name files clearly and include notes about intentional effects, edits or balances that must stay.

When does the turnaround begin?

The stated turnaround begins after the scope, files and project slot are confirmed. Mention a fixed release or manufacturing date in the first enquiry so feasibility can be checked before starting.

How do revisions work?

Each service states how many revision passes are included. Send one consolidated set of clear, time-coded notes per pass. Work outside the agreed scope is confirmed separately before it is started.

Can I request several delivery versions?

The versions included are listed on each service card. Ask about instrumentals, acapellas, club cuts, vinyl versions or other formats in the enquiry so the exact delivery set can be confirmed.

How do booking and payment work right now?

Selected fixed-price services have hosted PayPal checkout links. Services without a verified checkout link open a prefilled email enquiry, and quote-based work is confirmed by email before starting.
