    About

    Gabriel Garcia Alonso is a Berlin-based sound engineer, producer and live performer. He makes music with his brother as Lack of Fate, runs the label Fate Through, and works solo as Hologram People.

    The work begins by finding the record’s centre: what must hold, what can move, and what should remain untouched.

    Every decision is made by ear and in context—not to impose a generic finish, but to make the record’s own logic clearer.

    Where I can help

    The best fit is a record with a clear identity and one unresolved tension: translation, low-end hierarchy, movement, density, or the final handoff.

    I do not impose a new identity on a record that already has one. Arrangement, sound design, vocal editing, restoration, rush delivery, artwork and distribution are separate conversations. If the right answer is less intervention—or another specialist—I’ll say so.

    Before you send

    What should I send first?

    Send a private listening link, your deadline, one or two references, and a short note about what still feels unresolved. Full-resolution production files come later, after the scope is agreed.

    Does an enquiry create a booking?

    No. The first enquiry is non-binding. Fit, scope, delivery timing, revision rounds and the total price are confirmed in a written quote before you decide whether to proceed.

    When does the delivery window begin?

    The delivery window begins after the written offer is accepted, any agreed payment condition is met, and the required production files have been received and checked.

    How do revisions work?

    The written quote states the included revision rounds. Send one consolidated set of clear, time-coded notes per round. Work outside the agreed scope is confirmed separately before it begins.

    Can I request several delivery versions?

    Ask about instrumentals, acapellas, club versions, vinyl versions or other formats in the enquiry so the exact delivery set can be included in the quote.

    How does AirGigs differ from a direct enquiry?

    AirGigs is a separate marketplace route for the service shown on its live listing. AirGigs handles that listing’s current scope, price, timing, booking, payment, messages and platform terms.

    Let me hear where the record is now.

    Send a private link, one or two references, the service you are considering and the date you need it. I’ll answer plainly: whether I’m the right fit, what the work would involve and the next step.
