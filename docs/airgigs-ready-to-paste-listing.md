## Main description

```text
Hi, I’m Gabriel.

I mix and master electronic music from Berlin, with a focus on translation, low-end control, club playback and clean release preparation.

This service is for artists, producers and labels who want a track to feel finished without losing its original character.

Send me your current bounce, references, deadline and release format. I’ll confirm whether the project needs stereo mastering, stem mastering, full mixing, mix + master, or a simpler feedback pass before we start.

Good fit:
- Techno and electronic music
- Club-focused tracks
- Self-released singles and EPs
- Label demos or release preparation
- Artists who want direct, practical communication

Included:
- Clear listening and technical check before starting
- Balanced, release-focused processing
- WAV delivery
- 1 included revision for mastering
- 2 included revisions for mixing or mix + master
- Practical notes if something in the source file needs attention

Please send:
- Current rough mix or bounce
- 2 to 3 references
- Deadline
- Release format: digital, club/DJ, vinyl or all
- Stems/multitracks if booking mixing or stem mastering
- Notes for sounds, effects or balances that must stay

Not included unless agreed first:
Production, arrangement, vocal tuning, restoration, artwork, distribution, label administration or rush delivery.

Full current rates and service details:
https://audio.gabs-utilities.com/
```
