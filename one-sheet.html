EP mastering, stem mastering, vinyl pre-mastering and album work are listed on the full service page. This is a non-binding overview; scope, timing, and price are confirmed in writing before work starts.

Best fit

  • Techno and electronic music.
  • Low-end translation and club playback decisions.
  • Release-ready masters, mixes and final handoff.
  • Artists who want clear scope before work starts.

What to send

Start with a private listening link, references, deadline, track count, release format and what still feels unresolved. After written agreement, you receive secure handoff instructions. Requested mixing stems or multitracks should start at the same point; requested mastering files are normally 24-bit WAVs with headroom and no final limiter where possible.
