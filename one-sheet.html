How to begin

You do not need to know the technical diagnosis first. Send a rough mix, finished premaster, grouped stems or a private listening link, together with references, deadline and what must remain intact.

If you are learning, the first reply can help identify the right next step. If you are already experienced, send your usual files, naming and transfer method. Scope, rate, turnaround and delivery formats are confirmed before work starts.
