What to send

Start with a private listening link, references, your deadline and what still feels unresolved. After written agreement, you receive secure handoff instructions. Requested mixing stems or multitracks should start at the same point; requested mastering files are normally 24-bit WAVs with headroom and no final limiter where possible.

If you choose the wrong service, it can be corrected before work starts. The point is to make the first message clear enough to answer quickly.
