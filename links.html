What to send

Send a private listening link, rough mix, references and deadline. For mixing, stems or multitracks should start at the same point. For mastering, send a 24-bit WAV with headroom and no final limiter if possible. You can keep the file-transfer and naming workflow you already use.

If you are new to the process, you do not need a technical diagnosis before writing. If you already have a precise workflow, send the normal files and notes. The service route can be corrected before work starts.
