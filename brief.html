Before sending

Good to include

  • A private listening link or rough mix.
  • Two or three references.
  • A realistic deadline.
  • The exact delivery format you need.

Avoid sending first

  • Unlabelled stems or files starting at different points.
  • Full-resolution archives before the service is agreed.
  • Vague notes like “make it better” without references.
  • Rush deadlines without saying the release date upfront.
